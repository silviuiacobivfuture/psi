## 3. Clinical and legal review queue (P1)
Every page with a `CLAIM_*` or `TESTIMONIAL_*` flag: URL, flags, the exact sentence(s), suggested fix. Sorted by priority.

## 4. Contradictions
Facts that disagree across pages or sites (address, phones, emails, prices, team names and credentials, programme names, durations). For each: the values, where each appears, which one looks current, who must confirm.

## 5. Duplicates and merges
Groups of duplicate or near-duplicate pages, the chosen target for each group, and what unique content must be carried over.

## 6. Facts inventory
Counts by kind, and the full lists of: services and methods; conditions; programmes; prices; people with roles and credentials; contact data; trademarks; partners and equipment.

## 7. Gaps
Questions people will ask that no page answers (compare with the hubs in the target structure). Hubs with no source content (for example `/copii/logopedie`).

## 8. Redirect map
Count of redirects; confirm every old URL has a target or a reason to return 410. (The full map is `audit/redirects.csv`, generated by `pnpm run audit:redirects`.)

## 9. Open questions for the owner
Numbered, each answerable in one line.

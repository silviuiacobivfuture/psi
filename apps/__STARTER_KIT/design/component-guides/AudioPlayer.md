A session player: a large play button in `accent`, title and duration, and a waveform whose played part lights up in `glow-1` and breathes while playing.

Used for EQ-SYNC sessions, Hemi-Sync free exercises and the gated audio library. Without `src` it is a visual placeholder (click toggles the state).

- Provide: `title`, `src`, `duration` (seconds), optional `meta` (e.g. "Ziua 4 · Theta"), `progress` (0–1, for a static preview).
- Do: say what the session is for in `meta`, and remind listeners to use headphones where the method needs them.
- Don't: autoplay, or put the player on the ambient field.

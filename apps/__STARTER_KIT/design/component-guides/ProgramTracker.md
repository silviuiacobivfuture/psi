Progress through a multi-day programme (EQ-SYNC's 21 days by default): a grid of days, completed in `accent`, today ringed in `glow-1`, the count in mono.

It is the client-facing proof of "sesiune cu sesiune". Use it in the EQ-SYNC account area and for any Psizone programme with a fixed number of sessions.

- Provide: `total`, `done`, optional `title`, `note`.
- Don't: shame missed days; there is no red state.

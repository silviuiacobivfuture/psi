# Hemi-Sync România

The Romanian home of Monroe Institute® programmes: Gateway Voyage®, SyncCreation®, Lucid Dreaming®, free exercises, products, the Focus levels and the Clubul Delfinului community. The feel is **dawn, journey, contemplative**: a mountain at first light, mist in the valley, the quiet before the day. Palette and type come from hemi-sync.ro.

Themes: `hemisync-light` ("Dawn", primary) and `hemisync-dark` ("Night", for evening listening and the member area at night).

## Who it speaks to

Curious explorers of consciousness, meditators and people in transition. They want depth and seriousness, not mysticism for its own sake, and they value the Monroe Institute lineage.

## Voice

- Invitational and spacious; a little poetic, always grounded: "Călătoria ta spre interior începe aici."
- Second line of a headline in Fraunces italic to carry the emotion (as on the site: "Călătoria ta spre interior *începe aici.*").
- Name programmes and Focus levels precisely, with ® where licensed. Explain what a listener actually experiences.
- No claims of paranormal outcomes as facts; speak of exploration and experience.
- Hemi-Sync never appears on Psizone's clinical pages, and Psizone's guide never recommends it for clinical questions.

Real lines:
- Hero: "Călătoria ta spre interior *începe aici.*"
- Section: "Drumuri ghidate către conștiență." / "Începe cu exercițiile gratuite."
- Primary action: "Descoperă programele"

## Colour

- Dawn: white ground, `ink` #20242C, a lavender wash (`surface-2` #F3F0FF) for sections and selected states.
- `accent` is ink itself: primary buttons are dark pills with white text, as on the site.
- `glow-1` violet (#6A5CFF) is the brand light: links, the active Focus level, the played waveform.
- `glow-2` dawn peach and `glow-3` sky are decorative only (gradients and mist over photos), never text.
- Night: deep indigo ground, soft violet `accent` with dark text, peach focus ring.

## Type

Fraunces for headlines with italic accents (weight 300 for the italic line), **Inter** for text and interface, exactly as on hemi-sync.ro. Overlines in Inter caps with wide tracking ("INSTITUTUL MONROE · ROMÂNIA").

## Ambient

Almost still: a soft dawn gradient with slow mist (60 s loops). It is designed to sit over or beside landscape photography rather than replace it.

## Imagery

- Romanian landscapes at dawn and dusk (Piatra Craiului, mist over forests), wide and quiet, with room for type in the sky.
- People only small in the frame, walking, sitting, listening; never posed meditation stock.
- Never: galaxies, chakras rendered in neon, third-eye graphics, floating astral bodies.

## Signature components

`AmbientField` in mist mode over photography, `ProductCard` (albums, courses), `AudioPlayer` (free exercises), `Button` as ink pills, `GlassCard` over landscapes, `HubCard` for programmes.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Let photography carry the emotion | Fill the sky with text |
| Italic Fraunces for one phrase per headline | Italic body copy |
| ® on first use of each programme name | Drop or invent trademarks |
| Violet for links and active states | Violet buttons (buttons are ink) |
| Free exercises before the shop | Lead with prices |

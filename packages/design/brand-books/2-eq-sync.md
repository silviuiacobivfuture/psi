# EQ-SYNC

A 21-day guided sound programme for mental calm, built on binaural frequencies: "Liniște mentală ghidată de sunet". People sign up for a free programme, listen session by session, buy the full protocol and return in their account. The feel is **nocturnal, sonic and precise**: the quiet of a dark room at night, a pair of headphones, a warm light. The palette and type come straight from eq-sync.ro.

Themes: `eqsync-dark` ("Night", primary) and `eqsync-light` ("Day", for the account area in daylight, receipts and print).

## Who it speaks to

Adults whose minds stay "on the run" long after the stress has passed: shallow sleep, a nervous system on alert. They are sceptical of wellness fluff and want to understand the mechanism and see a clear path.

## Voice

- A promise with a structure: "Îți promitem un drum de 21 de zile, sesiune cu sesiune, spre o liniște mentală adevărată."
- Explain the mechanism in one plain line, then move on: "Fiecare ureche primește un ton ușor diferit; creierul recompune diferența."
- Concrete numbers that are true: days, minutes, sessions. Never efficacy percentages.
- Calm, intimate, a little understated. Short sentences. Night-time vocabulary: liniște, seară, respirație, somn.
- Always say when headphones are needed.

Real lines:
- Hero: "Îți promitem un drum de 21 de zile, sesiune cu sesiune, spre o liniște mentală adevărată."
- Problem: "Mintea rămâne „pe fugă” mult după ce pericolul a trecut."
- Primary action: "Vreau programul gratuit"

## Colour

- Night navy ground (`bg` #141C24) with cream ink (`ink` #EFE7D8): never pure white text.
- Gold `accent` and `glow-1` (#E3B24C) is the brand: the action, today's session, the played part of a waveform.
- `glow-2` teal (#5FA6B8) is the second sound wave and the focus ring; `glow-3` amber is warmth.
- Day theme flips to warm cream paper with a navy `accent` pill and deep gold as `glow-1`.

## Type

Fraunces for headlines (exactly as on eq-sync.ro), **IBM Plex Mono for body and labels**: it reads like a sound engineer's notebook and is the brand's most distinctive trait. Keep mono paragraphs short (3–4 lines) and at least 15px. Buttons and nav in mono at 13px with slight tracking.

## Ambient

Horizontal bands of light plus two thin sound waves in gold and teal, swaying slowly: the field looks like sound, not like weather. Stopped under reduced motion.

## Imagery

- Waveforms, headphones on a nightstand, a warm lamp in a dark room, hands on a chest breathing.
- Line-art and data in gold and teal on navy. The current site's glowing brain is acceptable but replace it over time with waveforms and real moments; it reads generic.
- Never: neon, sci-fi HUDs, glitch effects, brain scans used as decoration.

## Signature components

`AudioPlayer` (sessions), `ProgramTracker` (the 21 days), `ProductCard` (protocols), `AmbientField` in sound mode, `Button` in mono.

## Do and don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| Structure: day, session, minutes | Vague promises of transformation |
| Gold for one thing per screen | Gold text in paragraphs |
| Mono for body, Fraunces for headlines | A third typeface |
| Celebrate showing up ("6/21") | Streak-shaming or red missed days |
| Say "folosește căști" where needed | Autoplay audio |

The living background behind heroes and the AI guide. One component; each brand theme gives it its own feel through `glow-1`, `glow-2`, `glow-3` and `blur-ambient`:

- **Psizone:** three soft orbs drifting on 22–30 s loops (aurora).
- **EQ-SYNC:** horizontal bands of light plus two thin sound waves, swaying slowly.
- **Hemi-Sync:** almost still mist over a dawn gradient; meant to sit over landscape photography.
- **Blog:** barely there, no motion.

One per screen. Text inside stays short and large (`display-*`) or sits on `surface-glass`. All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

- Provide: `children`, optional `intensity` (`low` | `medium` | `high`), `style` for size.
- Don't: speed it up, add colours, or use it inside cards or behind long reads.

# Site research (11 November 2026)

What was found on the three current sites. psizone.ro was read through its WordPress API and in the browser; eq-sync.ro and hemi-sync.ro were looked at on their homepages only (the audit in `audit/` covers every page properly).

**Size (public API):** 95 pages, 23 posts, 244 WooCommerce products, 1 LearnPress course, 559 media files, 7 post categories (arhivă 5, Evenimente 3, Fără categorie 3, Grupuri de lucru 4, Noutati 16, Psihologie Sportivă 0, Workshop 3). About 34,000 words in pages and posts; about 15,000 of them clinical.

**Homepage (newest copy, Sept 2026):** H1 "Evaluare psihologică și intervenție multidisciplinară în Galați"; cards for Autism/TSA, ADHD, Integrare senzorială, Psihoterapie copii și adolescenți, Logopedie și autonomie, Kinetoterapie copii; sections on psychotherapy, QEEG and neurofeedback, team roles, a first-step CTA, then "REECHILIBRARE prin Hemi-SYNC®". Footer is entirely Hemi-Sync/Monroe links.

**Facilities (Concept page):** 2 psychotherapy rooms, 2 neurofeedback rooms, 1 brain-mapping room, 1 TMS room, 2 ABA rooms, 2 speech-therapy rooms, 1 group/workshop room, 1 multisensory integration room, 1 physical re-education and massage room. The April 2024 launch article listed a different set (3 psychotherapy rooms, neurofeedback and mapping, neurosensory integration, group room, a floatation tank) at Str. Alexandru Ioan Cuza 72.

**Team page:** Daniela Turcu (PhD candidate, psychotherapist, neurotherapist, administrator), Adrian Turcu (clinical psychologist, psychotherapist; Monroe Institute® trainer per workshop pages), Iuliana Mușat, Nicoleta Năfureanu, Marica Sîrbu, Georgiana Gheorghiță.

**Visual state:** two systems on one page: a dated navy OceanWP header with an ornate circular emblem logo and a starfield banner, over a newer teal-gradient homepage hero with a gold button. Headings Roboto Condensed, body Roboto. Colours seen: #173643, #147A74, #D7AA59, #EEF7F6, navy #265077.

**Problems found:**
1. Two businesses in one site: clinic and Hemi-Sync wellness/shop; page titles still say "Partener The Monroe Institute® | Reechilibrare prin Hemi-Sync®".
2. Name written four ways: PsiZone, Psizone, PSIZONE, "PSIZONE by Ioana Sârbu" (2024 launch). A Cluj clinic named PsihoZone exists.
3. Flat 25-item services menu mixing methods, conditions and audiences; ADHD appears twice.
4. Hype and unsupported claims ("cele mai avansate", "revoluționare", efficacy statements), duplicated paragraphs, typos ("înautism", "Deep Conection"), Lorem ipsum on the teen workshop page.
5. No pricing, no booking flow, no intake; Gmail as primary email; address inconsistent across sources (Al. I. Cuza 72 vs Gen. Alexandru Cernat).
6. 26 near-empty pages that each hold one audio track, several with pasted file URLs as slugs.
7. Three posts of exactly 1,581 words (likely duplicated body); duplicate event pages; an orphan "Camera Virtuală" page; leftover LearnPress and WooCommerce system pages.

**Products:** 244 Monroe Institute titles (categories include METAMUSIC® 102, MEDITATION 130, Wellness 95, Reduce Stress 92, RELAXATION 90, Mind Food® 74, Gateway Experience® 13, among others).

## Market notes (Romania)

- Local competition in Galați: individual practices and recovery centres listed on la-psiholog.ro and romedic.ro (Psizone listed with 10/10 from 2 reviews, hours 8:00–19:00, online booking link).
- National online therapy platforms: Hedepy (from Czechia, in 11 countries including Romania, sessions and subscriptions, app), PSIHOLOG.APP (launched Oct 2023), Calmly. None combines QEEG, neurofeedback, TMS and ABA under one roof, which is Psizone's distinctive position.
- Regulator: Colegiul Psihologilor din România (CPR) accredits psychologists; its ethics rules on advertising and testimonials must be checked before publishing testimonials.
